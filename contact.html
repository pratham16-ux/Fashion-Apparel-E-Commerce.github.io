FAQ

Common Questions

How long does shipping take?

+

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days domestically and 7–12 days internationally. Express options are available at checkout.

What is your return policy?

+

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery, provided items are unworn with tags attached. Refunds are issued to your original payment method.

Do you ship internationally?

+

Yes, we currently ship to over 42 countries worldwide with duties and taxes calculated at checkout.

How do I find my size?

+

Each product page includes a detailed size chart. You can also contact our concierge team for personalised fit advice.
