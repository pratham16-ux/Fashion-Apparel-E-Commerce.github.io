Who We Are

Designed Slowly,
Worn Forever

Stackly began in a one-room studio in Paris with a single conviction: that clothing should be made to last generations, not seasons. Fourteen years later, that conviction hasn't moved an inch.

We work with a small circle of family-run ateliers across Europe, choosing fabric mills for their integrity rather than their price sheet. Every piece is designed to be repaired, not replaced.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Craft First

Every stitch is checked by hand before a single piece leaves the atelier floor.

Responsible Sourcing

Deadstock, organic cotton, and traceable wool — nothing enters our supply chain blind.

Fair Partnerships

We've worked with the same ateliers for over a decade, paying above market rate.
